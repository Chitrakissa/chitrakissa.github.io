Who is making this?

I'm an engineer, and Chitrakissa is an idea I'm trying out: painting families' stories as films. There is no big company or server farm behind it. Every film is painted on my own personal computer, one at a time, about 12 an hour. So sometimes there's a queue, or you'll be asked to come back a little later. Thank you for being patient with it.

Your photos: upload them only if you're comfortable, and only of people who agree. I use them for one thing, making your film. I don't need them and I don't keep them: they're deleted the moment your film is delivered, and they never go to any AI company.

It's free. If you love your film, share it. If something isn't right, tell me. Your honest feedback is the real help.

What happens to your photos?
  • They are used only to make your film, on our own computer. They never go to any AI company.
  • They are deleted from our cloud and our computer the moment your film is delivered.
  • Your film and your order page delete themselves after 45 days. Your order page has a "Delete my photos now" button if you want it gone sooner.
  • Never used to train AI, never shared, never sold.
  • Photos of children only with a parent's OK.
