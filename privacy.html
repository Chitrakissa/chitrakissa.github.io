Our privacy promise

We ask for as little as we can, and keep it for as short as we can. In plain words:

  • No photos, no sign-up. Roshni needs only your greeting, your name and an optional line.
  • Kept for 30 days, then deleted. Your words and your film stay on your film's page (a link only you have) so you can download it.
  • Gifts keep nothing on our side. The names in a rose or Two Stars live only inside the link you send.
  • Never shared, never sold, never used to train AI.
  • We never ask for money, OTP or bank details. Everything we make right now is free. If someone asks you to pay in our name, it isn't us.

What we keep, and why

  • Your greeting, name and line: to make your film. Deleted after 30 days.
  • Your answers to our short questions (optional): to decide what we make next. Kept without anything that identifies you, unless you add your Instagram handle to hear from us.
  • A count of films per day: so the service stays fair for everyone.
  • Anonymous visit counts: which pages are opened and what is tapped, so we know what to improve. No cookies, no IP addresses, nothing that identifies you, and no other company involved.

Your rights

You can ask us anything, or ask us to delete anything, at any time: DM us on Instagram @chitrakissa with your film's link.
