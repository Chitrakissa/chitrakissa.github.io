The story behind it

To say "I see all of you."

Kitne Rang began as a surprise for someone very dear. Everyone who loves her knows her moods by heart, and loves every one of them, even the ones she would rather we forgot. So we painted them, one by one.

Each mood became a petal. The petals bloom one after another, a little story in each, and the flower turns until it opens on her at the centre. The film was made to make her feel special, to say what is hard to say out loud: I have noticed every side of you, and I adore all of it.

It turned out too lovely to make only once. So we are making it a film anyone can give.

"So many moods, and that is what makes you adorable."
