Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Roshni is free: make one for your family group, your friends, your colleagues. A tiny "chitrakissa.github.io" at the very end is our only ask.

Do you need my photos?

No. Roshni needs only your greeting and your name. Nothing else is asked or stored, and your film is deleted after 30 days.

How long does it take?

About two minutes. Your film's page shows when it is ready, and you can download it to send on WhatsApp.

Can I make more than one?

Yes, a few a day: one for each group you want to wish.
