Diwali, the way your region celebrates it

Four Diwalis, painted with respect.

North

Ram aaye Ayodhya: fourteen years of waiting end and the whole city lights its lamps, with Tulsidas's verse. Or Lakshmi ka aagman on haveli lamps and rangoli.

East

Akash Prodip: a lamp raised to the sky and fourteen lamps for the ancestors, the Hooghly ghats lit, with Tagore's "Aalo amar aalo". Or Lakshmi's footsteps on alpana.

West

Diwali Pahat: akash kandils before sunrise, the children's killa, aaji's faral, with Sant Dnyaneshwar's Pasaydan. Or Lakshmi's footsteps on rangoli.

South

The Deepavali Dawn: the dawn Narakasura's darkness ended, lamps and kolam, the oil bath before sunrise, with the Gita. Or Lakshmi's footsteps on kolam.

Verses are quoted exactly from their sources. We never show a deity, only the lamps and the traditions around them.

Questions

Who is making this?

I'm an engineer trying out an idea: painting families' stories as films. There's no big company behind it. Every film is painted on my own personal computer, about 12 an hour, so when many orders come in at once the order page shows a short queue or asks you to come back a little later. Please upload photos only if you're comfortable; I use them only to make your film and delete them once it's delivered. If you love it, share it, and tell me honestly what to improve.

Is it really free?

Yes, your first film is free, with a small "made with Chitrakissa" card at the end. If you want another one, it is ₹50, paid by UPI on your order page after you see a preview. We never ask for OTP or bank details.

What do you do with our photos?

We use them only to make your film, on my own computer. They never go to any AI company and are deleted once your film is delivered. Full privacy promise.

How long does it take?

Usually under 3 hours. The order page shows how many films are ahead of yours. Every film passes automatic checks and is looked at before it is sent, so on busy days it can take a little longer.

Which photos work best?

Clear faces looking at the camera, in good light. Original photos work better than screenshots or forwarded ones. If a photo doesn't work, we'll tell you which one.

Can I make one for my parents or friends too?

Each family's first film is free, so they can make their own in two minutes. Or make another one yourself for ₹50.
